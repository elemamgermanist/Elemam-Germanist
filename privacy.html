How requests are handled.

Last updated · 2026

This page describes how data submitted through elemamgermanist.com is handled.

What you send

When you fill out a learning request, the following is collected: first name, email address, city or country, written answers to two open questions, frequency preference, and optionally how you found this page.

Why

This data is read solely by Ahmed El‑Emam to respond to your request. It is not used for marketing, advertising, or shared with third parties.

Where it's stored

Submissions are stored through Tally, the form provider, and synced to a private spreadsheet readable only by Ahmed.

How long

Data is kept while your request is active. If you become a learner, data is kept for the duration of our work together plus twelve months. If you don't, data is deleted within six months of your last contact.

Deletion

You can request deletion at any time by writing to ahmed@elemamgermanist.com. Deletion takes effect within seven days.

Payment

Payments are handled privately through the payment method shared in the invoice. No payment data is collected on this page.

Analytics

This page uses cookie-free analytics that count visits without identifying you personally. No cookies are placed in your browser.
