◆The gap

Between what's on the page
and what's in the room.

Many learners spend years with German they can read, understand, and write.

Then a real conversation arrives — and the German that worked on paper stays on paper.

It isn't a competence problem. It's a structural one. And it can be closed.

The work begins where correct German stops being available.

◆Fit

Who this work fits.

This is for you if

  • — You understand or read German but can’t yet speak it comfortably.
  • — You’re an adult learner with real stakes: work, study, life, official requirements, family, or a coming exam.
  • — You want correction, structure, and rhythm — not just conversation hours.
  • — You’re a serious beginner who wants a real foundation, not a gamified app.
  • — You have an exam or certificate goal — and want the German underneath it to be real.
  • — You’re ready for a clear rhythm and a real direction — not a thirty-day promise.

This may not fit if

  • — You want a certificate on paper without the German behind it.
  • — You want a large anonymous class where you can stay quiet.
  • — You want disconnected hours instead of a clear learning rhythm.
  • — Your goal is to translate from another language word-by-word.
◆Methode

What lessons work on.

◆Register translation

The German you write and the German you say use the same words differently. We work on the difference, deliberately.

◆Structured correction

Correction is the material — not embarrassment. Mistakes are named, restructured, rehearsed, and put back into use.

◆Speaking under real conditions

Lessons rehearse the rooms where German actually happens: meetings, calls, doctor visits, family conversations — whatever your German has to survive.

◆Real material from outside the classroom

German that exists in the world: media, conversations, situations, texts that weren’t written only to be learned. The specific material is chosen for you.

◆FAQ

Questions that arrive most often.

How does pricing work?
Pricing depends on the format and goal — a first private session, a private package, exam-related preparation, or a beginner foundation. The right next step (and what it costs) is shared privately after your request is reviewed, so the offer fits the learner instead of fitting a price list.
What happens after I submit the form?
An automatic acknowledgment within minutes. The personal next step within one to two working days — written by Ahmed, with the format and details that fit your goal. Or a kind note if it isn’t the right fit.
Do you prepare for exams or certificates?
Yes, when the exam work is connected to real German: speaking, writing, expression, correction. The goal isn’t only to pass something on paper — it’s German that carries afterward.
Is this conversation practice?
No, not just conversation. Conversation happens, but the work is structured correction, expression, and making German usable in real situations.
What level of German do I need to start?
It depends on the goal. Beginners can start if they want a serious foundation. Speaking and exam-related work usually requires some German already in place. After your request, the format that fits is proposed privately.
How does payment work?
Payment details are shared privately after your request is reviewed. No public payment link appears on this page.
What if I miss or need to reschedule a lesson?
Scheduling rules are shared with you before payment. In general, reserved time needs respect: late changes may count as used, because the time was held for you. When something genuinely comes up, we handle it clearly and without drama.
What languages do you teach in?
German is the direction. English bridges where needed. Arabic can be used where it genuinely helps. The work moves toward German being the language we use most.
